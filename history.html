History

How this site changed.

Screenshots of the three finished versions of this website, from the first portfolio to today. Click a picture to open it at full size.

  1. 01Nov 2025 – Nov 2026

    The first portfolio

    A dark, game-style portfolio: a hub, the portfolio with skills and projects, services, a resume written as a quest log and the web apps, plus a second theme called "Enter the void". It is kept as a frozen archive and no longer part of the site.

  2. 026 – 10 Nov 2026

    A website that builds itself

    A new start: an empty page and one button. A click sends out cursor bots with jobs (layout, copy, design, accessibility, motion) that lay out, write and draw the page live, section by section while you scroll. Dark, with the CV as a page and a swarm of hundreds of agents as the finale.

  3. 0312 Nov. 2026

    Editorial

    The current design: light paper, Space Grotesk, a giant name and a box that travels to the corner and becomes the bots' home. Every page of the site now shares this one design.
