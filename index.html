About

Game design, 3D art and a habit of building tools.

I'm a game designer and co-founder of Moonflux Interactive. I shape the player experience and keep the technical and game design documentation that connects ideas to development.

My background is mixed on purpose: a commercial apprenticeship, a diploma in Game Art & 3D Animation at SAE Zürich and live operation of cinema technology at Cinema 8. I also build my own tools, like Dienstplan, a shift-planning web app with PDF import and Google Calendar integration, and this page.
