About

Game design, 3D art and a habit of building tools.

I'm a game designer and co-founder of Moonflux Interactive. Alongside, I build my own tools and interfaces that people really use. My focus is where design, technology and hands-on building meet: from the player experience and 3D art to web apps that save time and make a process clearer, in games as much as in everyday business.

My background is mixed on purpose: a commercial apprenticeship, a diploma in Game Art & 3D Animation at SAE Zürich and live operation of cinema technology at Cinema 8. I keep the technical and game design documentation that connects ideas to development.
